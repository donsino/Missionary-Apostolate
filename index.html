Our Sacred Calling

Our Mission & Service

Healing Ministry of Christ

To extend the healing ministry of Jesus Christ through compassionate physical care, prayerful emotional support, and crucial sacramental outreach directly in homes and clinics.

Restoring Human Dignity

In private visitation and hospital ministries alike, we strive to restore native dignity, kindle unshakeable hope, and bring spiritual peace in Christ to the darkest hours.

Spiritual Guidance & Wisdom

To be an enduring source of spiritual guidance, providing holy sacraments and warm companionship, reminding the suffering that their pain is never borne in isolation.

Intercessory Devotions

Let Us Pray

Prayer is the absolute engine of our service. Join us in raising these spiritual intentions before the Lord daily.

O Lord, bless the hands of our missionaries who travel far and wide to carry the healing touch of Jesus. Grant them resilient health, deep peace, and the gift of your holy wisdom. Guide their steps as they seek out the isolated and distressed.

Merciful Father, wrap your loving arms around all who suffer in body and spirit. Grant them comfort in pain, courage in fear, and the sure knowledge that they are never alone in their trial.

Lord, bless those whose generosity sustains this ministry. Multiply their gifts a hundredfold and grant them the same peace they help bring to others.

Holy Spirit, illuminate the path of every member of this apostolate. Grant us discernment in service and humility in leadership.

Father, bind us together in one heart and one mission, that our unity may be a living witness to your healing love.
